About Geotechnical Engineering

Geotechnical Engineering deals with the behaviour of soil and rock and their interaction with foundations, earth structures and infrastructure. At CUSAT, the M.Tech programme develops advanced understanding through academic study, practical exposure and research.

The programme is offered under the Division of Civil Engineering, School of Engineering, CUSAT. The curriculum connects advanced geotechnical concepts with analytical methods, field practice and research-oriented project work.

Technical capabilities

A practical mix of geotechnical fundamentals, engineering analysis, field interpretation and digital tools relevant to consulting, construction and infrastructure projects.

01 / FOUNDATIONS

Foundation Engineering

Foundation behaviour, bearing capacity, settlement and deep foundation concepts.

02 / GROUND

Ground Improvement

Soil modification, grouting, reinforced earth and geosynthetic applications.

03 / NUMERICAL

Numerical Analysis

Analytical and finite-element approaches for civil and geotechnical problems.

04 / INVESTIGATION

Site Investigation

Interpretation of subsurface information and engineering characterization.

05 / EARTHQUAKE

Earthquake Geotechnics

Liquefaction, seismic ground response and stability-related concepts.

06 / GIS

Spatial Analysis

GIS-based visualization and spatial interpretation of geotechnical information.

07 / ROCK

Rock Engineering

Rock mechanics, rock–structure interaction and tunnelling-related studies.

08 / DATA

Engineering Data Analysis

Data interpretation, statistical analysis, technical reporting and evidence-based engineering decisions.
